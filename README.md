## The story

> Every night I tuck my daughter into bed and leave her night-light on, so the dark won't scare her. Tonight she whispered that she isn't afraid of the dark anymore, because the man in the corner said he lives there now, and he has my voice.

- **Characters:** 239 (limit: 400)
- **Sentences:** 2 (exactly as required)

## Why it fits the brief

Sentence 1 is a calm, specific, domestic scene built around one concrete object — the night-light — a ritual meant to keep a child safe from the dark. Sentence 2 twists it: the daughter is no longer afraid, because the horror is already inside the room, it lives there, and it speaks with the narrator's own voice. The twist reframes the first sentence entirely — the night-light never protected anyone, and the threat may be the narrator himself. No gore, no explanation, no question: the dread comes from what the second sentence quietly reveals was true all along.
